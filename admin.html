Metronome click nudge

Milliseconds to shift the click earlier or later. Positive = click plays later; negative = click plays earlier. Doesn't change the tempo or the spacing between clicks, only when the very first one in the loop lands.

Square order

Which grid position each square appears in. Doesn't touch the video or audio — the same source footage just gets drawn into a different cell, so this is free to change any time.

Stem volume

Trim each stem's playback level in dB, relative to its normal (0dB) level. Applies instantly to anyone already playing this piece.

Notation images

The sheet-music strip shown under the focus square for each instrument. Upload a base image that's always used, and optionally extra images that only appear during specific bars — each one uploads and goes live immediately, separately from Save & publish below.
